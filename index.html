Discover the Cosmere

The Cosmere is an interconnected fantasy universe created by author Brandon Sanderson. It contains multiple worlds, each with its own history, cultures, characters, and magic systems.

Series such as Mistborn and The Stormlight Archive, alongside standalone novels like Elantris and Warbreaker, all take place within this larger universe.

You don't need to understand every Cosmere connection before you begin reading. Many of its stories are designed to be enjoyable on their own.

Cosmere Worldhopper is here to help you discover these books, follow a reading order that suits you, avoid unwanted spoilers, and track your progress throughout your reading journey.
